Why RiceFlow

Benefits of using RiceFlow

Made for rice farmers in Panabo City, Davao del Norte, using DA-PhilRice guides and real harvest data from the province.

Check your farm

Pin your farm on the map. RiceFlow checks the land, soil, rain and temperature for that spot.

Best rice varieties

See which NSIC varieties fit your farm and the season, with the reasons why.

Step-by-step guide

Follow the PalayCheck steps from seed to harvest. RiceFlow reminds you what to do and when.

GeoRice AI

Ask about your rice or a pest problem. Answers come from DA-PhilRice booklets and show the source page.

How it works

Your path to a better harvest

From signing up to harvest day, RiceFlow walks with you through the season.

1

Create your account

Pick your language and sign up. It takes about a minute.

  • English, Filipino or Bisaya
  • Your information stays private
2

Pin your farm and get your varieties

Put your farm on the map. RiceFlow checks it and shows the rice varieties that fit it best.

  • Land, soil and weather for your spot
  • Expected yield for each variety
3

Follow the planting guide

Do each PalayCheck step and tap Log this step when you finish. Tap Explain this step if you need help.

  • Reminders before each step
  • Help for pests and diseases
4

Track your crop to harvest

See your crop's stage, the expected harvest date and your yield, season after season.

  • Yield estimate from Davao del Norte harvest data
  • Your yield history
Install

Install RiceFlow in 4 steps

RiceFlow is not on the Play Store yet, so you install it from this website. It takes about 2 minutes.

  1. Download

    Tap Download for Android on this page. If your phone asks, tap Download anyway.

  2. Open the file

    When it finishes, tap Open. You can also find RiceFlow.apk in your Downloads or Files app.

  3. Allow the install

    If you see "For your security…", tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  4. Install and sign up

    Tap Install, then Open. Create your account and add your farm.

Only download RiceFlow from riceflow.me. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway. It shows this for apps that are not from the Play Store.
Questions

Good to know

Is RiceFlow free?

Yes. RiceFlow is free to download and use.

Which phones can use it?

Android phones with Android 7.0 or newer. There is no iPhone version yet.

Does it need internet?

Yes. RiceFlow needs mobile data or Wi-Fi to check your farm, show the weather, save your logs and use GeoRice AI.

Where does the advice come from?

The planting guide follows DA-PhilRice PalayCheck. GeoRice AI answers from DA-PhilRice rice booklets and shows the source page. For serious problems, also talk to your City Agriculture Office.

How do I update the app?

Download it again from this page and install it. Your account and farm data stay saved.
